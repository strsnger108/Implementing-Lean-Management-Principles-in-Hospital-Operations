Synergy Lean Hospital

White-label mobile platform that eliminates hospital waste, shortens length of stay, and gives patients real-time visibility into their care journey.

The Problem: Operational Waste in Indian Hospitals

🔴 35% Non-Value-Added Time

Patients spend over one-third of their hospital journey waiting — for admission, diagnostics, and discharge — consuming bed capacity and increasing costs.

🔴 Consultant Bottleneck

40% of inpatient cases concentrate with a single doctor, creating single-point-of-failure risk and uneven resource utilization.

🔴 Discharge Delays

Average Length of Stay (LOS) is rising — from 3.03 to 3.72 days in 4 months — without standardized discharge workflows.

Current State: Value Stream Waste

The Solution: 3-Role Mobile Platform

🤒

Patient App

Hospital code + OTP signup. Real-time status tracking, discharge alerts, and 1-click feedback mapped to Lean waste categories.

👩‍⚕️

Staff App

Kanban admission board, discharge tracker, consultant workload Pareto, digital 5S audits, and Kaizen idea submission.

📊

Admin App

Hospital branding, consultant roster, configurable patient journey stages, Lean metrics dashboard, and PDF/CSV reports.
